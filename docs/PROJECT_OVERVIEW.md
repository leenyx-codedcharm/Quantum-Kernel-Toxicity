# Project Overview

## Aim

Evaluate classical, quantum, and hybrid quantum-classical approaches for binary hERG inhibition prediction using curated molecular data.

## Scope

The project has four connected research tracks:

1. **Classical molecular learning:** ECFP4, RDKit descriptors, molecular similarity, and conventional classifiers.
2. **Quantum representations:** fidelity kernels and quantum reservoir computing.
3. **Hybrid variational learning:** PennyLane variational circuits with trainable classical readouts.
4. **Quantum-chemical information:** GFN2-xTB electronic descriptors, subject to coverage and provenance checks.

## Research principle

All comparisons must be methodologically matched. A model is not considered superior because it uses a quantum circuit. Predictive benefit must be demonstrated on the same frozen partitions with selection isolated from final testing.

## Current interpretation

The classical baselines currently outperform the exploratory quantum and hybrid models. The research therefore investigates the conditions under which quantum representations may help, rather than presupposing quantum advantage.
