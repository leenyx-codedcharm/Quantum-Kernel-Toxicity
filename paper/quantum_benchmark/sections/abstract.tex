\begin{abstract}
We compare fixed and trainable simulated quantum kernels and variational quantum
classifiers with Tanimoto-SVM on a matched 64/128/128 molecule pilot from the
frozen hERG\_Karim scaffold\_r0 split. Test labels are not used for feature fitting,
model selection, or threshold selection. Completed results are reported without
claiming quantum advantage.
\end{abstract}
\textbf{Keywords:} quantum machine learning; molecular toxicity; hERG; quantum kernels
